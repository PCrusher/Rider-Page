      FAQ

      Good to know
      How can I sponsor you?

      Send a short message with what you have in mind. I work with equipment, nutrition and travel partners, and I share a media kit with race calendar, audience numbers and rates.

      Which races will you ride next season?

      The calendar is still being planned. The main goals are the Swedish Gravel Championships and two multi-day marathon events. Dates will be posted on the blog as soon as they are confirmed.

      What gear do you ride?

      A gravel bike with 40 mm tyres for most races and a hardtail for marathon events. Wheels, clothing and nutrition come from the partners listed above.

      Can I use your photos or videos?

      Yes, for editorial use with a photo credit and a link back to this page. For commercial use, please write to me first.

      Do you give talks or run training camps?

      A few times a year. I run weekend gravel camps in Västra Götaland and speak at clubs and schools about endurance training and race nutrition.

      Want to partner up?

      Race calendar, audience numbers and rates are in my media kit. Send a message and I will reply within two days.
